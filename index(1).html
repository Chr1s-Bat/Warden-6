    Field manual

    Warden-6 is a field command console for small units. It helps a unit leader plan missions, read intelligence about allied nations, keep a personnel roster and watch supply levels, all in one place.

    Intended users: team leaders, operations clerks and logistics staff who need quick, readable status on a phone or a laptop.

    Problem it solves: unit information is usually scattered across notes and radio calls. Warden-6 keeps it in one list-based interface with clear warnings and safe deletes.
